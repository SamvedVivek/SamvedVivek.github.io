About

I am a final year Data Science student at UW-Madison with a Computer Science minor, after a B.S. from Manipal Institute of Technology. My work spans data science, quantitative finance, and ML research, and I am drawn to the messy edge of machine learning, where models meet noise, adversaries, and data that does not behave.

I like taking a problem all the way through: framing it, building the model, and getting it deployed where people can actually use it. The projects above are where that shows up in practice.
